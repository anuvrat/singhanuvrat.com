## Quick Thoughts

- Claude Code is impressive (but expensive).
  - I finally played around with Claude Code this week. (Late to the party, I know!)
  - **The friction:** Even on the Pro plan, I hit rate limits within an hour. The disruption to my flow made it clear this isn’t quite ready for heavy individual consumer use yet, or perhaps I just haven’t calibrated my sense of token cost.
  - **The insight:** My workflow naturally shifted from _reviewer_ to _orchestrator_. Instead of reading every line of diff, I found myself defining guardrails and setting a vision for the agent to execute. I was moderately successful, but it forced me to define "skills" in a much more structured way. There is so much more to explore here.
- **Tooling Shift:**
  - **Grok:** I’m slowly switching to Grok for deep technical exploration. I’m finding its responses preferable for the "hard stuff."
  - **ChatGPT Study:** Still my go-to for scaffolding new topics.
  - **Gemini:** Essential when my personal context (emails, docs) is part of the query.
- **One wish:** I really want **ChatGPT Atlas** to succeed. The browser-agent concept is too good to fail.
